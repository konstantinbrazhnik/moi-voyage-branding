# MoiVoyage — the network

The public face of the house. Curators share the places and moments they love
on a journal of their own, travellers follow the curators whose taste they
trust and find the places those curators have stamped, and curators sell
voyages as packages with pages. The passport remains the product; the network
is how people find a reason to hold one.

Decided 7 November 2026. Mockups: [`network/mockups.html`](./network/mockups.html).

## 1. What it is, in brand terms

| | |
|---|---|
| The analogy | Substack for the relationship (a curator's journal, free to follow, paid to support), Pinterest for the surface (photographs first), Yelp's job (find a place) with the opposite ranking (who stamped it, not how many strangers rated it). |
| The clients | Curators. They sell packages; the house takes its share and says so on the receipt. |
| The users | Travellers. Following is free. Supporting a curator is paid and unlocks letters and asking them directly. |
| The promise | Nobody pays for a stamp. No venue placement, no ratings, no "sponsored". Ads, if ever, are declared as ads and never look like stamps. |
| The verb | **To stamp.** A curator stamps a place they love. A traveller stamps a place to keep it. A place's standing is which curators stamped it. |

## 2. The stamp family

Three stamps, one construction (a ring, a monogram, a tilt, an ink of its own):

| Stamp | Whose | Ink | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| The house stamp | MoiVoyage | house red (light tint on dark) | the cover, the souvenir |
| The voyage stamp | a voyage | its template's accent, or its curator's ink when sold as a package | the passport page, the souvenir |
| The curator's stamp | a curator | their own ink, chosen once from the house set (Seine, grass, brass, wine, heather, and more as needed) | on every place and moment they stamp, large on their journal |

A partner's stamp (an agency, an operator) is square, so a business can be
told from a person at a glance. Its rules are in `BRAND.md` §8.

## 3. Rules the network adds

1. **Photos are loud, chrome is quiet.** The house palette stays. Colour comes from photographs and stamps.
2. **The house never says "I".** Curators speak in the first person, in the hand, at 24px and up, one line at a time. Facts stay in Lora on the grid.
3. **No ratings.** A place has stamps and lines, never stars or scores.
4. **Every ranking is explainable in one line**, shown under the block: from curators you follow; near you; most stamped this year; places in common.
5. **Disclosure rides on the stamp.** A comped meal or stay is declared on the stamp itself and shows wherever the stamp shows.
6. **Comments live on letters only.** Moments and stamps are not debated.
7. **Ask answers only from stamps.** It attributes every line, says how many curators and how recent, and says when nothing fits.
8. **Urgency only when true.** "Six of twelve pages left" is allowed because a voyage has a fixed number of travellers. Nothing else counts down.

## 4. The screens

| # | Screen | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Latest (signed out) | The thesis as content: the newest stamps, as photographs, with the curator on each. |
| 02 | For you (signed in) | From curators you follow, near you, new stamps, curators to follow, and your next voyage if you have one. |
| 03 | A moment | One photograph, one line, three actions. Swipe up for the next. |
| 04 | Places | The directory: occasions as filters, stamps as pins, curator lines as the summary. |
| 05 | A place | Who stamped it and why, the order, the facts, stamp it or add it to a voyage. |
| 06 | A curator's journal | Moments, places, letters, packages. Follow free; support paid. |
| 07 | An itinerary | A story in days that one button turns into a voyage. |
| 08 | A package | A voyage with pages for sale. |
| 09 | Ask | A question answered from stamps. |
