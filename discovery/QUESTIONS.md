# MoiVoyage · the brand questions

Nineteen questions in five groups. The six marked **decide first** are the ones
the three directions fork on. Each question carries the assumption the draft
directions were built on; answer by number, or correct the assumption.

## A. Who it is for

1. **Who is the first traveller MoiVoyage is built for?** *(decide first)*
   Assumption: friends and friends-of-friends first, paying strangers in view.
2. **What shape of trip is the home case?**
   Assumption: leisure trips of three days to three weeks, two to twelve people.
3. **How is it paid for, if at all?**
   Assumption: paid per voyage. One trip, one price, one world.

## B. What it is

4. **Is MoiVoyage one brand, or a house that makes a world per trip?** *(decide first)*
   Assumption: a house with a per-trip skin, recognised by its mark and manners.
5. **Is the assistant the product, or a feature of it?** *(decide first)*
   Assumption: the app is the product; the assistant is its most important
   feature and has a name that may change per trip.
6. **How much does it do without being asked?** *(decide first)*
   Assumption: it notices and suggests; nothing is booked, moved or paid for
   without a tap.
7. **What will it never do?**
   Assumption: never invents a fact, never pretends to have acted, never nags,
   never sells unasked.

## C. The voice

8. **Does it say "I"?** *(decide first)*
   Assumption: the house says "we" sparingly; inside a trip the named assistant
   says "I".
9. **Where is the dial between warm and witty?**
   Assumption: dry rather than jokey; amused, never sarcastic about the traveller.
10. **Does it have taste?**
    Assumption: taste learned from the traveller, and it says where a suggestion
    came from.
11. **How does it speak when something goes wrong?**
    Assumption: calm first, specific second, sympathy in one clause at most.
12. **How French is it?** (In French it would be *mon voyage*; *moi, voyage*
    reads as a wink.)
    Assumption: the name is the only French; "Moi" is used the way English
    speakers use it, a little theatrical.

## D. The look

13. **Physical-world metaphor, or digital-native?** *(decide first)*
    Assumption: physical at the trip level; the house stays quieter.
14. **Illustration, photography, or neither?**
    Assumption: drawing is a signature of the house; every voyage gets at least
    one drawn thing.
15. **How loud?**
    Assumption: quiet house, loud when the trip asks for it.
16. **What must it never look like?**
    Assumption: never a chatbot, never a booking site, never a dashboard.

## E. The name and what comes next

17. **How is the name set?** (MoiVoyage, Moi Voyage, moi·voyage, MOI VOYAGE;
    domain and trademark unchecked.)
    Assumption: one word in prose; the wordmark is free to split it.
18. **When it finds deals and books tables, how should money feel?**
    Assumption: transparent. It says what it found, what it costs, and who
    benefits.
19. **What does success look like in a year?**
    Assumption: dozens of trips for people you know, a handful for people you
    don't. Personal, not mass.

## The three directions, in one line each

| | Brand is | Who speaks | One line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · The Atelier** | a house that makes a world per trip | the trip's own narrator | "Every trip gets its own app." |
| **B · Moi, the companion** | one character, in every trip | Moi, always, in the first person | "Moi handles it." |
| **C · The Desk** | a concierge service you carry | the desk, in short declaratives | "The desk has already called." |

The full treatment of each, with palette, type, phone mockups and the same five
lines of copy in each voice, is in `brand-directions.html`.
