The stamp

A double ring, the name running round it, the monogram in the middle, pressed at eight degrees. Red on light surfaces, the light tint on dark ones. The app icon is the only upright use.

stamp #B8322F on field: 5.4:1

The voyage stamp

Each voyage is stamped in its template's accent, never in house red. Name in the hand, dates in Jost capitals, pressed between minus six and plus six degrees. It appears on the voyage's page and on the souvenir.
